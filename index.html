How to play

1Tap a car to drive it forward. Swipe it along its lane to slide it either way. Cars slide until they hit something.
2A car leaves only nose-first, when the lane ahead is clear. Letters board the yellow bus in word order (seat 1, 2, 3...); wrong letters wait in the holding bay.
3If a car leaves while the bay is full, the level fails.
⇄Scramble stops (purple bus): letters board in any order.
WBay Word: three wrong letters in the bay that spell a word (any order) leave for 10 bonus coins.
Finish a level to bank its fares (1 coin per letter, doubled at par). Spend coins on boosters, but you never need them.
